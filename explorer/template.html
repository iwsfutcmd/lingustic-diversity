What is measured

  1. Distance, not time. LDND on ASJP 40-item lists (Wichmann et al. 2010): Levenshtein distance normalized by word length, divided by the same measure for different meanings to remove chance resemblance. About 0 for identical lists and about 1 for unrelated languages.
  2. Diversity that ignores duplicates. Z = exp(−k·LDND), then the Leinster–Meckes maximum diversity D_max(Z): the effective number of fully distinct lineages. Adding another doculect that is identical to one already present does not change it.

Reading it

  • The calibrated scale is coarse. Two unrelated lineages (LDND ≈ 1) are still about 0.7 similar at k_cal, so their combined D_max stays small. The Between lineages view therefore also reports a finer scale (k = 3), where distinct lineages should add up.
  • Same-language doculect pairs differ by LDND 0.11 / 0.30 / 0.57 (10th / 50th / 90th percentile). Lineages in the lowest band cannot be ranked against each other.
  • D_max is stable against duplicates, not against sampling more distinct varieties. Lineages with few doculects are lower bounds.
  • Contact shortens distances. The 40-item list resists borrowing but is not immune.
  • Doculects listed in config/exclusions.tsv are left out of every number here, each with its reason. scripts/audit.py flags candidates (far from the rest of the lineage, fewer than 30 of 40 concepts, or removing it lowers D_max by 0.015 or more) but never excludes automatically. A deep branch such as Chuvash is flagged and kept; a list distorted by contact or a transcription problem is excluded.
  • Samples: up to 80 doculects per lineage for diversity and profiles, 40 for the concept resampling, 25 for between-lineage comparisons, chosen round-robin across genera and Glottocodes.

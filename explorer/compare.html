Distance between two varieties

Pick each variety by ISO 639-1 or ISO 639-3 code, Glottocode or name. The distance is computed here, in your browser, from the ASJP v21 40-item word lists, with the same PMI-LDND as the rest of this page. A code can match several word lists (several transcriptions of one language, or the members of a macrolanguage such as zh), so every pair of lists is scored and the median is reported.

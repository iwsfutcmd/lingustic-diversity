isodiv

How internally diverse is a language lineage? A measure that needs no clock and does not depend on how many languages or dialects you count, computed from 11,535 ASJP word lists.

How the numbers are madeFrom a 40-word list to Dmax, step by step, on real Japonic, English, German and Basque lists.

The idea

Distance in amount of change, not time. Two ASJP 40-item word lists are compared with PMI-LDND: Levenshtein-style alignment scored by learned sound correspondences, divided by the same measure between words of different meanings to cancel chance resemblance. No time depth and no rate of change are assumed.

Diversity that ignores duplicates. Distances become similarities, Z = exp(−k·d), and a lineage is scored by the Leinster–Meckes maximum diversity Dmax(Z): the effective number of fully distinct lineages. Adding another copy of a variety already present cannot change it, so it does not matter how finely a language is split into dialects. The scale k is set so two lists of the same language are 0.9 similar (k = 0.463).

What it shows

  • The bands do not overlap: isolates (Basque, Haida, Koreanic, Ainu) sit near one list, genus-sized families (Japonic, Germanic, Turkic) in the middle, deep families (Uralic, Indo-European) above. Every major Sinitic group scores at genus level.
  • Dmax measures diversity, not relatedness. Ten random unrelated lects score 1.48, and a union of unrelated families scores like Altaic or "Khoisan". Real families score below an unrelated set of the same size.
  • It tracks lexical change, not age. Oceanic outscores Indo-European at every sample size, and Formosan, where Austronesian's deepest splits are, scores lowest of its groups.
  • At the calibrated scale the deepest families all sit near the ceiling of about 1.56, and larger scales (the explorer's scale profiles) separate them.
  • Single-list isolates (Sumerian, Etruscan, Elamite, Hruso, Kusunda, Nihali) are within chance of every lineage. There is no Elamo-Dravidian signal.

Method and source

The README has the method, every result and the caveats (the same-language noise floor, sampling, contact). Related work is in docs/related_work.md. Everything here is regenerated by the pipeline in the repository; python scripts/distance.py jpn eng gives the same distances as the Compare tab.

Word lists: ASJP Database v21 (Wichmann, Holman, Brown, Dryer & Ran 2025), CC-BY-4.0. Maximum diversity: Leinster & Meckes 2016. LDND: Wichmann et al. 2010. Learned alignment scores after Jäger 2013, 2018. Built 2026-10-31.
